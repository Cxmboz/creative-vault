# Grounded design ideas

Ideas drawn from the first reference batch (Nov 8, 2026). Each idea names its sources and
whether it is historical or an artistic addition. Nothing here is decided; these are options
to pull into a weapon's spec.

## Ideas that apply to every weapon

| Idea | Origin | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Colorway system.** One shape, several colorways (lacquer, fitting metal, cord). In Roblox: same mesh, swapped texture set, so rarity tiers or skins cost almost nothing | Process | [[Owner attachment - Katana colorway set]] |
| **One motif, placed once.** A single motif in one zone of the scabbard (about a quarter of its length); the rest stays plain | Historical | [[Met 24378 - Katana mounting with pampas-grass lacquer]], [[Pin - Tachi mounting with single gold dragon]] |
| **Three-material palette.** Each weapon uses three main materials, each with one job, plus one accent | Historical | [[Met 27932 - Jian with jade hilt and green sharkskin scabbard]], [[WargateDirector - Light-Infantry Longsword]] |
| **The cord carries the accent color.** Grip cord, scabbard cord or tassel is where the bright color lives | Historical | [[Met 24378 - Katana mounting with pampas-grass lacquer]], [[sam1027 - Chinese Sword Design (Han and Tassel Jian)]] |
| **Ornament through texture.** Fish-roe dots, stone-texture lacquer, sharkskin, wood grain: surface richness from normal and roughness maps instead of engraving | Historical | [[Met 26069 - Shakudo collar with fish-roe ground]], [[Pin - Higo koshirae with stone-texture lacquer scabbard]], [[Pin - Tang jian with rosewood scabbard and brass fittings]] |
| **Cohesion by construction.** The sword and scabbard share a material or wrap (grip wrap continuing onto the scabbard, matching fittings), not just a color | Historical | [[Met 894754 - Itomaki-tachi slung sword mounting]] |
| **Silhouette first.** Guards and whole weapons are tested as flat black silhouettes, then as a strip of 8–12 variations, before detail | Process | [[CreativeDyslexic - Tsuba Designs No.2]], [[SRegan - Backsabre silhouette strip]] |
| **Palette strip on every spec.** The spec renders its colors as swatches, not just codes | Process | [[BrunoPinto112 - Katana Design Sheet]] |

## A new weapon class to consider

**Twin swords in one scabbard.** The Chinese double sword holds two flat-backed blades in a
single scabbard. For a combat game this is a distinct class: a split draw and a dual-wield
move set, fully grounded in history. Source: [[Met 27929 - Double sword sharing one scabbard]].

## Deliberately not taking
- Flaming, glowing or energy blades (effects work, and the futuristic track)
- Geometric guard shapes on Japanese weapons (triangles, stars)
- Gold paint over whole blades
