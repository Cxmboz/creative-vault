# Cliché list

Allowed only with a stated reason in the spec.

- Random gemstones set into guards, pommels or blades
- Glow or emissive effects by default
- Skulls, demon faces, generic monster heads
- Filigree or engraving covering every surface
- Decorative spikes and hooks with no function
- Fake runes or pseudo-script engraving
- Color-coded elemental glow (fire red, ice blue, poison green)
- Oversized blades out of proportion to the grip
- Symmetry everywhere, including where the tradition is asymmetric
- Wings, feathers or flames as guard shapes

Add entries when a review catches a new one.
