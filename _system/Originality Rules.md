# Originality rules

The vault stores reasons, not looks.

1. **Principles over pictures.** Cards record construction, function, proportion and the
   tradition's rules, not designs to copy.
2. **Triangulate.** Every new design draws on at least three references from different
   sources, at least one a real historical object, unless the concept is the owner's own.
3. **Lineage.** Each design decision in a spec names the reference that informed it.
4. **Cliché check.** Every spec is checked against [[Cliche List]]. Items on it need a
   stated reason tied to theme or construction.
5. **Ornament where the tradition puts it.** Quiet surfaces stay quiet; restraint is recorded.
6. **Construction must make sense.** Every part can be explained: held, mounted, sheathed.
7. **Distance check.** At review, compare against the references and the rest of the
   collection; a near copy or a sibling look is revised.
8. **Trace only public domain.** Contemporary concept art sets mood only.
