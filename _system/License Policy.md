# License policy

| License | `reuse` | In a model or texture | In a published game |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CC0, public domain | free | Yes | Yes |
| CC-BY-4.0 | credit-required | Yes | Yes, with credit in the game's credits |
| CC-BY-SA | credit-required | Only if the derived asset may be shared alike | Avoid |
| CC-BY-NC, CC-BY-ND, CC-BY-NC-SA | reference-only | No | No |
| Proprietary, concept art, unknown | reference-only | No, inspiration only | No |
| Paid assets, ripped or unclear-origin files | do-not-use | No | No |

- Anything traced, projected or baked into a model must be `free` or `credit-required`.
- Credits for `credit-required` items are collected in [[Credits]].
- When in doubt, mark `unknown` and treat as `reference-only`.

## Artistic references (DeviantArt, Pinterest, concept art)

- Images may be kept as **private study copies** in `_private-refs/` inside the vault. That folder is
  ignored by Git, so it stays on the owner's PC and is never uploaded or shared. Embed them in notes
  with `![[file]]`. Never move them into `Attachments/`, the asset library, or any published file.
- Always record the creator. Pinterest pins are often re-posts: trace to the original where
  possible, otherwise record who pinned it and mark the creator "unidentified".
- Take **individual ideas** (a material pairing, a placement rule, a silhouette move) and
  develop our own version. Never reproduce a whole design.
- If a creator states terms (for example "NO AI" or "do not re-upload"), record them in
  `creator_terms` and honor them: such works get a credit-and-link note only, with no
  design analysis.
- Skip works labeled AI-generated; they reproduce the generic look the arsenal avoids.
