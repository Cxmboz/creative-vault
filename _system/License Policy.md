# License policy

| License | `reuse` | In a model or texture | In a published game |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CC0, public domain | free | Yes | Yes |
| CC-BY-4.0 | credit-required | Yes | Yes, with credit in the game's credits |
| CC-BY-SA | credit-required | Only if the derived asset may be shared alike | Avoid |
| CC-BY-NC, CC-BY-ND | reference-only | No | No |
| Proprietary, concept art, unknown | reference-only | No, mood and study only | No |
| Paid assets, ripped or unclear-origin files | do-not-use | No | No |

- Anything traced, projected or baked into a model must be `free` or `credit-required`.
- Credits for `credit-required` items are collected in [[Credits]].
- When in doubt, mark `unknown` and treat as `reference-only`.
