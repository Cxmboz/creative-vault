# Pin - Higo koshirae with stone-texture lacquer scabbard

![[ref-pin-higo-koshirae.jpg]]

Inspiration only. Private copy in `_private-refs/` (on this PC, never uploaded); original at the source link.

Photo of a real antique mounting. The Japanese caption reads roughly: Higo-period mounting with stone-textured lacquer scabbard, with a Higo-school Nishigaki openwork paulownia tsuba with inlay.

## What caught our attention
The scabbard is ishime lacquer: matte and stone-textured. It's interesting to look at with no ornament at all. The openwork paulownia tsuba is the only decorated part.

## Elements
| Element | Origin | How we could use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ishime (stone-texture) lacquer | Historical | Roughness and fine normal map on the scabbard; a matte alternative to gloss |
| Openwork paulownia (kiri) tsuba | Historical | Pierced-guard test for J01 (a Tier 2 capability) |
