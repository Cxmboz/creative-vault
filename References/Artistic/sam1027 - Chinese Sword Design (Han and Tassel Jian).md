## What caught our attention
Two jian side by side with opposite moods. The left has a dark blade with a gold rhombus lattice, a red cord-wrapped grip and a gold guard with a key-fret motif. The right is pale and quiet: a light blade, a silver beast-mask guard, a ridged wooden grip and one pair of pale cyan tassels.
