## What it teaches
A ring pommel, and a long slim scabbard articulated by metal bands and suspension mounts. The bands give the scabbard rhythm without surface decoration.

## Elements
| Element | Origin | How we could use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ring pommel | Historical | Alternative pommel for a Chinese weapon; also a tassel anchor |
| Scabbard banded by fittings at intervals | Historical | Rhythm on long scabbards instead of all-over ornament |
