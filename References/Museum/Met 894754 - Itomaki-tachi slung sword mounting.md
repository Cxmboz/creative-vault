## What it teaches
The upper scabbard is wrapped in the same braided thread as the grip (itomaki means thread-wrapped), so the sword and scabbard read as one object by construction, not by matching paint. Gold-flecked lacquer below, openwork guard, and hanger fittings for wearing it slung edge-down.
